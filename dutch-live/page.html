Before the show

  • Open this page in Safari, not inside another app.
  • Set Settings -> Display & Brightness -> Auto-Lock to Never for the evening. The page stops when the screen locks.
  • Rehearse tonight: play a Dutch stand-up clip from a laptop across the room and watch the delay figure at the bottom.
  • Try once with AirPods connected and once without, and compare how much of the Dutch line comes out right. With AirPods connected, the iPhone may listen through the AirPods' microphones, which are aimed at your own mouth.
  • In the theater, use Pocket to black out the screen. Tap twice quickly to come back.
